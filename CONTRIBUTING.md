# Contributing

Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are welcome. For anything large, open an
issue first so we can agree on the approach.

By submitting a pull request, you agree that:

1. the contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit it;
2. you give the Forkbell copyright holder a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free,
   irrevocable licence to use, change, sublicense and distribute your
   contribution under any terms, including commercial ones; and
3. you keep the copyright in your contribution.

This keeps one person able to publish Forkbell in app stores, offer it
commercially, or change its licence later without tracking down every
contributor.
